Estimate the cost of a product 3D model from the deliverable you need. Count capture, modelling or generation, corrections and the export you will actually use. Compare options by cost per approved model. A low cost per attempt can become expensive when several attempts fail the same acceptance check.

![Editorial rendering: game controller, half solid and half wireframe](../covers/3d-modeling-cost.webp "Editorial illustration made in Blender.")

## What do you put in the quote?

Name the target application, format, materials, number of variants and whether animation or editable source is required. Give the modeller enough photographs and one clear reference for each feature you will inspect. Ask what revisions and licensing the quote covers.

## How do you compare costs across methods?

Divide the total spent on a small pilot by the number of models you can use. If a hypothetical four-item pilot costs 120 currency units to produce and 40 currency units to review and fix, and three models pass, the cost is about 53.33 currency units per accepted asset. This arithmetic is an example, not a rate.

## What should you check before publishing?

| Method                  | Main line items                              |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Photo-based generation  | Attempts, review and corrections             |
| Commissioned modelling  | Brief, hours or fixed quote, revision rounds |
| Existing supplier asset | Licence, cleanup and re-export               |

## FAQ

### Should I budget for revisions?

Yes. Agree on the number of rounds and what counts as a correction. Include your team’s review time in the pilot, even if it does not appear on the invoice. Ask how a new product variant is billed and whether fixes for a missed requirement use up a round.

### Can I use one quote for the whole catalogue?

Start with representative simple and difficult items. Build estimates per group after recording how many assets pass and what usually needs correction. A complex chair with thin pieces and reflective materials can take substantially different review time from a plain vase.

### What if we already have CAD?

If you already have CAD, check reuse permissions and confirm which details are needed. Prepare CAD for a web viewer by cleaning up geometry, assigning materials, and exporting separately. Verify your CAD file’s format and export settings match the web viewer’s requirements.
